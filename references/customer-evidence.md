# Customer evidence — mining customer records for proof accounts

**Goal:** attach named, real proof accounts to each claim so assertions are checkable and rep-usable. An assertion with a named account behind it is a different object than a bare assertion.

## Method
1. Query your **customer intelligence records** for accounts matching the brief's segment (HQ, entities, old stack/incumbent, competitors, win drivers, trigger event).
2. For each claim in §6, find the account(s) whose real history substantiates it. Pull the trigger-event narrative and the pain-point detail; note the POCs.
3. Rank: identify the **keystone accounts** — the one or two that substantiate multiple claims (these are also the best case-study/permission-ask targets).
4. Add a **proof-accounts column** to the §6 claims table; thread named citations into §1 (ICP portrait), §3 (personas — real people per role), §4 (triggers, why-us wins), §5 (hero quotes), §8 (objections — solved patterns).

## Two disciplines, held every time
- **Proof-of-pain vs. proof-of-outcome.** Most customer records capture *why they moved* (the problem) — cite that freely. Few capture *what improved after go-live* (the result). NEVER attach an account to an outcome/ROI claim the record doesn't support. Keep the outcome-evidence gap visible; it's usually the missing half of the brief.
- **Quote clearance.** Naming an account as internal *evidence* is fine. Publishing its verbatim quote externally is a separate permission gate — flag every verbatim as clearance-pending. Check whether logo/quote rights already live in the account's MSA before assuming you need a fresh ask.

## Restraint
Citations are phrases, not paragraphs. `(Real: Acme Co — broke scaling into three new countries.)` beats a sentence. The brief should read as tightly cited, not as a case-study anthology.

## Also capture market-context threads
Some accounts aren't proof of a single claim but validate the whole thesis (e.g. a channel partner independently stating the market dynamic, or a referral-network pattern proving "buyers trust peers"). Put these in the brief's market-context/§5 area, not forced into a claim row.
